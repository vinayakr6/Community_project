Common online threats

Understanding how attacks work is the first step toward preventing them. Be alert to these common techniques.

🎣
Social engineering

Phishing

Fake emails, texts, or websites designed to trick you into revealing passwords, payment details, or personal information.

🦠
Malicious software

Malware & ransomware

Harmful software that can steal data, spy on activity, damage devices, or lock files until a ransom is demanded.

🔑
Account security

Password attacks

Attackers exploit reused, weak, or exposed passwords to access accounts and move between services.

Your everyday safety checklist

Small actions, repeated consistently, can significantly reduce your exposure to common cyber risks.

🔑Use unique passwordsUse a password manager and never reuse important passwords.
📲Enable MFAAdd a second verification step to important accounts.
⬆️Update regularlyInstall operating system, browser, and app security updates.
🔎Verify firstCheck links, requests, QR codes, and unexpected attachments.
📶Use trusted networksAvoid sensitive transactions on untrusted public Wi-Fi.
💾Back up your dataKeep important backups and test that they can be restored.
🙈Share lessLimit personal information and review app permissions.
🚩Report suspicious activityTell your institution, service provider, or relevant authority.

Pause. Verify. Protect.

When something feels unusual online, stop and verify through a trusted channel.
